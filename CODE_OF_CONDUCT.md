# Code of conduct

We want UltraSearch MCP to be a practical, respectful, and technically serious project.

## Expected behavior

- Be direct and kind.
- Keep reviews focused on code, safety, docs, and user impact.
- Respect maintainer decisions on scope and release timing.
## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment or personal attacks.
- Publishing private keys, tokens, or user data.
- Pressure to bypass safety controls or provider billing guardrails.
- Spam, brigading, or off-topic promotion.

Maintainers may remove comments, close issues, or block contributors who violate this policy.
